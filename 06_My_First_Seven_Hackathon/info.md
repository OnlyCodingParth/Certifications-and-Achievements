## 05. Google Cloud — Build with AI: Code for Communities

### 📌 Project Development | Kisan Alert AI

![Build with AI Hackathon](06_My_First_Seven_Hackathon\06-Buil_with_AI_Code_for_Communities_Hackathon.png)

### 📖 About the Hackathon

As part of the **Google Cloud Build with AI: Code for Communities** hackathon, I worked on a project called **Kisan Alert AI**.

The project focused on exploring how Artificial Intelligence and digital technologies could support farmers by providing useful agricultural information.

The idea was to help farmers access timely updates related to weather conditions, crop health, and market trends.

Through this project, I explored the potential of technology-driven solutions for improving agricultural awareness, productivity, and sustainability.

### 💡 Project: Kisan Alert AI

**Project Objective:**

To develop an AI-oriented agricultural assistance solution that helps farmers make more informed decisions through timely information.

### 🌱 Proposed Features

- **Weather Alerts:** Provide useful weather-related updates.
- **Crop Health Information:** Support awareness of crop conditions.
- **Market Trends:** Help farmers stay informed about agricultural market developments.
- **Agricultural Assistance:** Explore technology-driven support for farming decisions.

### 🎯 Key Takeaways

- Explored the application of AI in agriculture.
- Gained project development experience.
- Practiced approaching real-world problems through technology.
- Developed a greater interest in socially impactful applications.
- Recognized the importance of accessible digital solutions.

**Outcome:** Kisan Alert AI Project Development
